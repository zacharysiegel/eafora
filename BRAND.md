# Brand

## Name

**Eafora** — Old English noun meaning *son, descendant, heir*. Chosen for its
literal etymological fit (the project is about generational continuity) and
because it is uncommon enough in modern English to be uniquely ownable.

## Pronunciation

Default to **/eɪˈfɔːrə/** ("ay-FOR-uh") for public use. The historically faithful
Old English pronunciation is closer to "AY-uh-vor-uh" but is not realistic to
teach to a general audience.
